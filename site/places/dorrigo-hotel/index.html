Stop 01 · a place and its people

Heritage Hotel Dorrigo

A century-old hotel can tell a story about migration, family enterprise and the social life of a country town.

Look up at the building

Michael Feros, a Greek migrant, built the hotel in 1925. A NSW Government announcement on 14 May 2026 reported its addition to the NSW State Heritage Register. The NSW Government describes it as a lasting example of the contribution of migrant communities to country towns.1

1925

The year the current hotel was built by Michael Feros. The Feros family has remained closely associated with the place for more than a century.1

Michael and Elene Feros

More than a building

Over the decades, the hotel hosted celebrations and fundraisers as well as everyday encounters. A 1941 ball raised money for the Greek War Fund. These details suggest how one building can connect local life to wider histories of migration and war.1

What other stories might a photograph, menu, invitation or family memory tell about this place?

What the museum could add

These sourced photographs offer a starting point. A completed trail entry could add a present-day view, link a museum object or document, and include a short recollection with the speaker’s permission. Additional collection material has not yet been selected or cleared for publication. The museum and hotel owners would review any installed marker and final story.

About this demonstration. The hotel history is supported by the NSW Government source below; photograph dates, identification and credits are linked in the captions. The interpretation, layout, QR marker and walking trail are examples for discussion; they are not an official museum or hotel publication.
